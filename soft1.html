Questions founders ask us first

Who owns the code and the designs?

You do. Everything transfers to you on final payment, and we set up your cloud, app store and domain accounts in your name from the start.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, before you share anything sensitive. We can use your format or ours.

Where will my data be stored?

In India by default, on cloud regions in Mumbai or Hyderabad, unless you tell us otherwise. We build with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act in mind.

Can we speak in Hindi?

Yes. We work in Hindi and English, and calls run on Indian Standard Time.

How do payments work?

You pay in milestones tied to working software you have seen. We accept UPI and bank transfer, and we share a written invoice for every payment.

What happens after launch?

You get 30 days of free bug fixes. After that, choose a care plan or pay per change. Either way you are never locked in.
